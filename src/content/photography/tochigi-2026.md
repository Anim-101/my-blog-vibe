# 栃木県 2026

"The world is a book, and those who do not travel read only one page." - St. Augustine.

And to read page after page of the book called world, I as a human, keep travelling, where I am completely lost in the translation of Mother Nature.

No matter when, where & how I travel, all of these things pile up to the main story of me as a human. With this thinking, I went to lost in the Mother nature, and this time it was Tochigi ken. My 22nd Prefecture in Japan.

As the number of my visited prefecture grows, the craving for this soul to know more, to see more, and to understand more about life grows as well.

I never thought I would discover myself in a new way after traveling to Tochigi. But with all of the experiences I had in the last two days, it was a life-changing experience for me. Taking a solo trip sometimes may sound a little bit off from the normal trend of traveling in a group.

But I would argue that on a solo trip, you are with your best buddy: yourself. The joy of seeing the waterfalls and the sculptures, and talking to them when no other being surrounds you—that is the most soul-soothing moment one could ever ask of life.

And I had the privilege to have this solo trip. I laughed, smiled, and talked to Mother Nature in a way I have never done before. I showed my emotions in every way so that Mother Nature could sense them in a way she has never sensed before.

Tochigi, you are the most beautiful place I have visited yet. Perhaps you allowed me to understand myself in exactly the way I needed to. This was my first time traveling to you, but certainly not my last. Thank you, Tochigi, and thank you, life, for the beautiful gift of this solo trip.

Just a solo traveller, who loves to lost in translation
Just a Mother Nature lover,

- Anim
